Reflection and thoughts

What have I learned?

At the start of the project I had the general knowledge of AWS which came from the courses completed on the earlier years. These courses did not go through all of the services AWS has to offer, but they gave very good basic understanding.

Now looking back at the starting position, I learned a lot of new things about AWS, but also from software development and all of the parts that belong to it. AWS Elastic Beanstalk was a new service that I got to learn and I think I got the hang of it pretty good. Overall the automation process with Github Actions was also new skill learned and I think it will be valuable asset for the future. I did have some knowledge on IaC from previous courses, which helped me to understand the CI/CD process better.

Frontend development was actually not my role, but I got to learn about that too. I learned about Angular and its structure and used Tailwind framework to produce frontend designs based on the UI designs we made. I got to develop my skills further in HTML structures and CSS. Accessibility and end-to-end testing became a little bit more familiar to me through the developing process. My handwork can be seen in the Login page and also in the Postpage; I re-structured the HTML code to fit the design needs and styled them accordingly.

Overall doing software development as a team with a project angle has become way more clearer to me. The processes and project structures are things that can only be learned by trying them out yourself. Looking back at the start, I am now way more competent and ready to work as a professional in future project settings.
