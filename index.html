    • Project Elli

    Project Elli is a software development project that started in JAMK Ticorporate study course. The lenght of the project was approximately 4 months.

    Elli's purpose is to work as a medium between the companies JAMK and Elisa to help them collaborate more easily and efficiently. The idea is to be a simple and easy-to-use collaboration tool which provides a hub for both parties to see what possible collaborations there might be available and who are assigned to them. The project's initial goal is improving the communication and collaboration between companies.

    Our team consisted of 6 students who had their own area assigned in the project. Team Elli followed the agile development with SCRUM methods for the whole duration of the project schedule.

    My main role in Project Elli was to be a Cloud Expert. This meant that I was in charge of planning and implementing the cloud Infrastructure according to the software's planned needs. My secondary roles were UI/UX design and content creation.
